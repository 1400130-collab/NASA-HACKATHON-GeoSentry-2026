    08Radar check

    An independent check on the labels, from NISAR

    The failure scars were found in Sentinel-2 optical images, and nobody has checked them by hand yet. NISAR was not used to find them, which makes it an independent witness. For each scar, each old forest clearing (already bare before the storm) and 5,000 random places, we compared radar coherence across the storm pair with the ring of ground 240 m around it.

    Descending orbit, pair …. Scars against old clearings p = …. On the two ascending orbits the scars still differ from random places (p ≤ …) but not from old clearings. The earliest pair, …, shows …% against …% at random places (p = …). Three orbits were tested. After a Bonferroni correction (×3), scars still differ from random places on the descending orbit, but scars against old clearings and the 21 July signal no longer pass 0.05. Read them as supporting evidence that the scars are real ground changes, not as a detector. Script: analysis/radar_scar_check.py.
