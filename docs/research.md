# What past Space Apps winners had in common (draft)

Based on NASA's published summaries of the 2024 and 2025 global winners and one winner's project page. The summaries do not say why the judges chose each project, so the patterns below are our reading.

## Judging

Five criteria: impact, creativity, validity, relevance, presentation. This comes from a search summary of the project submission guide; the official judging guide page would not load when we checked.

There are ten global awards, each rewarding a different strength. In 2025 there were 10 winners from more than 11,500 submissions.

## Patterns

| What they did | Example |
|---|---|
| Served one real person making one real decision | 2plant told farmers whether to plant now (2024 Galactic Impact) |
| Reduced NASA data to a single score or answer | Astro Sweepers gave every space object a risk index (2025 Galactic Impact) |
| Picked one specific place | QUEÑARIS targeted water scarcity in one Peruvian city (2025 Local Impact) |
| Went one step past the brief | SkyShield turned an asteroid-orbit viewer into satellite risk zones (2024 Best Use of Science) |
| Shipped a complete, usable thing | Nearly all were finished web apps, dashboards or platforms |

## Astro Sweepers in detail

Their project page shows the winning submission contained almost no machine learning:

- The risk index was a hand-weighted formula with four parts and fixed weights chosen from sensitivity tests.
- One worked case study: a single old rocket body scored before and after a small deorbit burn, going from failing NASA's rules to passing.
- Auto-filled NASA compliance documents as a ready-to-use output.
- Scenario numbers to 2100, a business model and a policy proposal.
- An image classifier described as exploratory and not integrated into the submission; anomaly detection and neural networks were listed as a future roadmap.

## What this means for us

- A transparent score, one convincing real example and a usable output document matter more than model count.
- We are still putting trained models in the backend (see [concept.md](concept.md)), but each one needs a clear job and an honest accuracy figure.
- Relevance is scored, so NISAR and surface change must stay central.
- Presentation is a fifth of the score. One person should own design, the demo video and the pitch from the start.
