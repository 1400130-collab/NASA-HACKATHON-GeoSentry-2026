# Outreach plan and interview questions (draft)

## Who to talk to next

- **County and city disaster-safety or forestry offices in North Gyeongsang** (Uiseong, Andong, Yecheon). These are the actual users.
- **Korea Forest Service** landslide prevention staff.
- **KIGAM** landslide researchers.
- **University labs** in geotechnical engineering or radar remote sensing. Email grad students as well as the professor.
- **Mentors at the Seoul event** who work in Earth observation or disasters.

At a main switchboard, ask for the person in charge of landslides: "산사태 담당자분 연결 부탁드립니다."

## Rules for the call

- Ask about what actually happened, not whether they would like our tool.
- Do not describe the tool unless they ask.
- When they mention a specific event, ask for the date and place.
- Write down their exact words, and ask permission before quoting.

## Opening

> 안녕하세요. 이번 주말 서울에서 열리는 NASA Space Apps Challenge 해커톤에 참가하는 학생입니다. 위성 레이더로 산사태를 감지하는 연구를 하고 있는데, 엉뚱한 걸 만들기 전에 현장에서 실제로 어떻게 일하시는지 듣고 싶습니다. 10분만 경험을 여쭤봐도 될까요?

*Hi, I'm a student in the NASA Space Apps hackathon in Seoul this weekend. We're working on landslide detection from satellite radar, and before we build the wrong thing I'd like to understand how this works in real life. Could I ask about your experience for ten minutes?*

## Questions, in priority order

1. 가장 최근에 관할 지역에서 산사태가 났을 때 얘기를 들려주실 수 있을까요? 처음 이상하다는 걸 언제 아셨고, 첫 한 시간 동안 어떤 일이 있었나요?
   *Tell me about the last landslide in your area. When did you first know something was wrong, and what happened in the first hour?*
2. 아무도 주시하지 않던 사면이 무너진 적이 있나요? 지금 돌이켜보면 징후가 있었을까요?
   *Has a slope ever failed that nobody was watching? Looking back, were there signs?*
3. 경고가 얼마나 일찍 오면 실제 대응이 달라질까요? 한 시간, 하루, 일주일 중에요.
   *How much warning would actually change what you do: an hour, a day, a week?*
4. 관리하시는 사면이 몇 곳 정도이고, 그중 1년에 직접 점검할 수 있는 곳은 몇 곳인가요?
   *How many slopes are you responsible for, and how many can you inspect in a year?*
5. 주의보나 대피를 내렸는데 결과적으로 필요 없었던 적이 있나요? 그 뒤에 어떤 일이 있었나요?
   *Have you issued a warning or evacuation that turned out unnecessary? What happened afterwards?*
6. 어디를 살펴볼지 정할 때 지금 무엇을 쓰시고, 가장 답답한 점은 무엇인가요?
   *What do you use now to decide where to look, and what frustrates you most about it?*
7. 2025년 산불 이후로 걱정되는 사면이 달라졌나요?
   *Since the 2025 fires, has it changed which slopes worry you?*
8. 새로운 도구를 실제로 믿고 쓰시려면 무엇이 증명되어야 하고, 누구의 승인이 필요한가요?
   *What would a new tool have to prove before you'd act on it, and who would have to approve it?*

## Closing

- 제가 여쭤봤어야 하는데 놓친 질문이 있을까요? *What should I have asked that I didn't?*
- 저희가 또 누구와 얘기해 보면 좋을까요? *Who else should we talk to?*
- 말씀해 주신 내용을 발표에서 인용해도 괜찮을까요? 원하시면 익명으로 하겠습니다. *May we quote you, anonymously if you prefer?*
